Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built for drone pilots, event operators, and anyone who needs to get a live video signal from point A to everywhere else.

🎯

Full-screen projection

Project your stream to any connected monitor or TV at the click of a button. Perfect for displaying drone footage on a big screen at events.

📶

Universal RTMP input

Works with DJI drones, DJI RC controllers, OBS Studio, and any device that can push an RTMP stream. No special drivers or apps needed on the sender.

🌐

Go live anywhere

Push your drone feed straight to YouTube, Facebook, Twitch or any custom RTMP server. Paste the server URL and stream key from your platform, hit Go Live. If the drone drops out, the stream picks back up when it reconnects.

⏺️

Local recording

Record streams to MP4 on your local drive. Switch recording on per stream, and choose where recordings are saved.

🔑

Multiple stream keys

Manage multiple stream keys with individual names and settings. Each key has its own recording, Go Live and NDI settings.

🔄

NDI output

Send any drone stream out as an NDI source on your local network, ready for vMix, OBS, TriCaster and other studio tools.

🔔

Auto-projection

Optionally project the stream fullscreen as soon as it connects — no need to be at the keyboard. Ideal for unmanned display setups.

🔁

Auto-updates

Checks for new releases automatically and offers a one-click update. Every download is verified before it installs.

How it works

Three steps from launch to live.

1

Install and add your stream key

Download and install RTMP Projector. Add a stream key — the app generates a unique RTMP URL automatically. Point your drone or encoder at it.

2

Start the server and fly

Hit Start Server. The moment your drone connects, the stream appears in the app. It opens fullscreen on your chosen display monitor, or click Open Projection Window to show it yourself.

3

Go live (optional)

Paste the server URL and stream key from YouTube, Facebook, Twitch or any RTMP service, and press Go Live. Viewers watch on that platform.
